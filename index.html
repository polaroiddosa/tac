What Awaits You

The TAC Camping Experience

More than just camping — it's a full reset for your mind and soul.

Sketching Workshops

Bring your charcoal, pens, and tablets. Engage in masterclasses focused on capturing perspective, shade, light, and natural textures in the wild.

Biophilic Walks

Guided explorations highlighting biomimicry and structure. Learn how nature constructs patterns, spirals, and load-bearing geometries.

Vernacular Studies

Visit remote forest communities and local settlements to examine native construction techniques, natural materials, and sustainable design.

Starlit Critiques

Pin up your sketches or share designs around the bonfire. Get feedback from experienced mentors and peers in an open, relaxed environment.

Geometric Living

Experience modern modular glamping. Sleep in architecturally interesting geodesic domes designed to maximize efficiency and connect with the terrain.

Design Networking

Connect with fellow architects, planners, and spatial designers. Exchange ideas, build relationships, and collaborate on future creative concepts.
